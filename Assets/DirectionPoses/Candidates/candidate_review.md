## Review caveat

Because the available real photos do not reveal every hidden surface, the body turn remains intentionally restrained. The head-neck perspective and near/far shoulder visibility carry more of the direction change than the hindquarters. This is preferable to inventing unreliable coat regions or producing a false side pose.
